## Failure injection

In a separate copy of `verification/`, the first entry of `phase_exponents_mod4_q6` in `flux_gauge_certificate.json` was changed by +1 mod 4 and `plot_face_graph_flux_sweep.py` was run on the tampered certificate. It exited with status 1 and reported two failures: "certificate q = 6 exponents have residue 1 mod 4 on every plaquette" and "the lift and the certificate gauge are isospectral at q = 6". The failure path works.
